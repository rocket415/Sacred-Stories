Every individual has meaningful stories and a fascinating history that lives inside of them. When their voice is gone, their stories go with them.

Sacred Stories exists to preserve them.

I’m a full-time podcaster who has conducted hundreds of long-form interviews over more than 10 years.

Using a professional setup, I record high-fidelity, one-on-one conversations with parents, grandparents, and loved ones — capturing their lives in a way that feels natural and real.

The result is a permanent archive your family can return to — not just as memories, but as a voice.

Who This Is For

For people who want to preserve the story of someone they love — a parent, grandparent, family member, or friend — while they’re still here to tell it.

Why It Matters

Photos fade. Details blur. Stories disappear.

This is a way to hold onto what matters — in the most human form possible.
